Entry for the [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118)
bounty on make.fun: one image summing up the world news of Wednesday
18 November 2026.

## The image

A 1024x1024 infographic, drawn programmatically with crisp rendered text —
no AI-generated lettering. Six stories, verified against reporting from
18 November 2026:

1. **Gulf strikes** — US jets hit an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island;
   Iran fired drones at Bahrain and Kuwait.
2. **Crimea bridge** — Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge a second day; a road
   span fell into the strait.
3. **Shenzhen summit** — Xi hosts Putin at APEC ahead of the first
   Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral on Thursday.
4. **Ebola in DR Congo** — UN: over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths as the
   outbreak more than doubled in three months.
5. **Nvidia** — revenue doubled to $115.8bn, the first results since
   co-founder Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO.
6. **Cyclone Xavier** — Category 5 storm batters the Northern Cook Islands,
   gusts to 315 km/h.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when set), byte for
byte, so both runs produce identical output. No network.
